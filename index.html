Crossing Indigenous Lands: Mission and Empire in the Rockies, 1811–1858

Five places where fur traders, missionaries and a British survey party travelled through country long known to Indigenous peoples. Switch the basemap (top right) to see elevation as contours and shaded relief. Select a marker or card to explore.

Reading the source critically

The source text calls the Rockies an “unknown country” and a “primeval” wilderness. They were unknown only to outsiders: Indigenous guides led nearly every journey mapped here, and the trails followed were already Indigenous routes. The mission, treaty and railway changes that followed are part of this map’s story.
